# DoctorWood privacy policy

*Last updated: 23 November 2026* · Italian version: [crslabs.github.io/privacy/doctorwood.html](https://crslabs.github.io/privacy/doctorwood.html)

DoctorWood is built to **collect no data**. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking services and no servers of its own.

## What data it handles and where it stays
- Everything you enter (customers, jobs, quotes, prices, stock, calendar, surveys, assembly sheets, company details) and photos stay **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.
- With **sync** (optional) jobs travel **only between your paired devices**, on the same Wi‑Fi, encrypted (TLS 1.3), or as encrypted files in a folder you choose. They don't go through any DoctorWood server. The workshop tablet receives only the jobs to make: title, customer name, installation address and date, parts and materials; never prices, phone numbers, emails or VAT numbers.
- **Backups** are encrypted with a password you choose and go only where you decide.

## When the app uses the internet
- **Update check** (Android app only; can be turned off in Settings → Updates): once a week the app reads from GitHub which is the latest published version. None of your data is sent; GitHub, like any website, sees the device's internet address. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- **Report a problem or an idea**: the app prepares an email; you send it yourself, with your email app, and only then does it reach crslabs.info@gmail.com. You can see the whole text first, including the technical information (version, device, latest errors: no names, amounts or photos), which can be removed.
- **Support DoctorWood**: opens the Ko-fi page in the browser only if you tap it. Any donations are handled by Ko-fi with PayPal or Stripe, under their own policies.

## Android permissions
- **Camera**: for survey and sheet photos, and to scan QR codes (pairing, labels).
- **Fingerprint / PIN**: to open the app and protect the owner's data, if you turn it on.
- **Notifications**: for sync (the tablet staying reachable during working hours, transfers in progress).
- **Local network**: for direct sync with your paired devices.
- **Install apps**: to install updates (Android always asks for confirmation).

## Deleting your data
Settings → Data and backup → «Cancella tutto da questo dispositivo» (erase everything from this device), or uninstall the app.
